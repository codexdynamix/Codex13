---
name: Cross-mode visual direction
description: Design constraint for the Codex Dynamics public site and CRM.
---

Use opaque, calm surfaces and clear borders across the public site and CRM in both light and dark modes. Avoid glass blur and decorative glow.

**Why:** The user said the CRM was still far from the desired restrained Apple-like feel and specifically asked for no glass or glow, while noting that many surfaces and text colors became too bright, faint, or invisible when switching modes.

**How to apply:** When adjusting appearance or theme behavior, favor neutral surfaces, legible foregrounds, visible but quiet borders, and verify both light and dark modes. Do not add translucent blur or ornamental glow.
